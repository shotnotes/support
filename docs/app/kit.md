# Your kit

The **Kit** tab holds what you shoot with, so that you choose it when you log a
shot, not type it each time. There is a list for each kind:

- **Lenses**
- **Filters**
- **Lighting**
- **Others**, for accessories

Add an item with the add button of its list. Tap an item to see the shots that
used it, or to edit it.

## A lens

A lens has:

- a **manufacturer** and a **lens name**;
- one or more **focal lengths**, in millimetres. Give one for a prime lens, or
  each fixed focal length of a lens such as a Tri-Elmar;
- an **aperture range**, the widest and the narrowest f-number. It is
  optional, but give both ends or neither. When it is set, a shot with this lens
  can only have an f-number from the widest to the narrowest;
- a **serial number**, engraved or printed on the lens, and a **description**.

The aperture range and focal lengths are checked against the shots you have
already logged: the app will not narrow a lens so that a shot taken with it
would no longer fit, and tells you how many shots are in the way.

## Filters, lighting and others

A filter has a **thread size** in millimetres, and a light has a **guide
number**. Each kind of kit has a manufacturer and a name, a serial number and a
description like a lens.

## Manufacturers

Lenses, filters, lighting and accessories share one list of manufacturers, so
that you type a name once. A manufacturer cannot be deleted while any kit
uses it.

## Deleting kit

Deleting asks nothing, and a bar with **Undo** is there for a few seconds.
Kit that shots use cannot be deleted: change the kit of those shots first. Kit
you delete can be brought back from **Settings**, **Recently deleted**.

## Sharing kit

Tap **Share this item** in the form of a lens, filter, light or accessory to
share it with another person by a QR code or a link, and use **Add from a
link** at the top of the Kit tab to add what someone shared with you. See
[Sharing kit](sharing-kit.md).
