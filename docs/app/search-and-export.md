# Searching and exporting

## Search

The **Search** tab finds shots by lens, kit, location or the words in your
notes. It matches all the words you type, so "35 harbour" finds the shots with
both words. Tap a result to open the shot.

## Exporting

Exporting saves shots in a file, with the extension `.snexport`, that the
[metadata writer](../writer/overview.md) reads to write the notes into your
image files. The file is named by the day of the shots, for example
`shot-notes-2026-10-16.snexport`.

You can export:

- the shots you choose: tap **Select** at the top of the Shots list, choose them,
  then **Export**;
- a whole day: **Export this day**;
- the shots a search found: **Export these**;
- a day, a range of days or all shots: **Settings**, then **Export shots**.

The phone's share sheet opens with the file. Send it to your computer the way
you usually do: AirDrop, a cloud drive, email, a cable. The app cannot tell
whether the file arrived, so check that it did before you delete anything.

## How many shots you can export

Recording shots is free, always, and so is the export of up to 24 different
shots a month. A shot counts once in a calendar month, however many times you
export it, and the month starts again on the 1st by your phone's clock. Before
it exports, the app tells you how many exports you have left, and how many of
the shots you chose count against them. When you have fewer left than you
chose, it offers to export the shots you have already exported this month, the
first few, or lets you choose which.

![The question before exporting one shot, with how many exports are left this month](../screenshots/app-export-confirmation-light.svg#only-light){ .phone loading=lazy }
![The question before exporting one shot, with how many exports are left this month](../screenshots/app-export-confirmation-dark.svg#only-dark){ .phone loading=lazy }

If you close the share sheet without sharing, the shots are not counted.

A paid plan, Plus, is planned that lifts this limit. It is not available yet.
