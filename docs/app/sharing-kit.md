# Sharing kit

You can share a lens, a filter, a light or an accessory with another person
who uses Shot Notes, by a QR code or a link. It is free, needs no account and
sends nothing to a server. Only kit can be shared, not shots, and one item at
a time.

## Share an item

1. Open the **Kit** tab and tap the item.
2. Tap **Share this item**, at the bottom of its form. The page shares the item
   as it is saved, so if you have changed the form, tap **Save** first: the
   button waits until you do.
3. The other person scans the code with their phone's Camera app. Or tap
   **Share link** and send the link with Messages, email or any other app.

![The page that shares a lens: a QR code, the link and the Share link button](../screenshots/app-share-kit-light.svg#only-light){ .phone loading=lazy }
![The page that shares a lens: a QR code, the link and the Share link button](../screenshots/app-share-kit-dark.svg#only-dark){ .phone loading=lazy }

The code and the link hold the item's kind, manufacturer and name, and the
details that go with its kind: the focal lengths and aperture range of a lens,
the thread size of a filter, the guide number of a light. They do not hold your
shots, places or notes, or the description of the item.

### The serial number

A serial number is left out unless you choose to send it. If the item has one,
the page has an **Include serial number** box. It starts as **Settings** says,
under **Include serial number when sharing**, which is off to begin with. You
can change the box for one share without changing the setting.

### When an item is too big

A long name or serial number can make the link too long for a QR code. The page
says so, and still gives you the link to send as text.

If something in the item is longer or larger than a link can hold, such as a
name of more than 200 characters or a serial number of more than 100, the page
says that the item cannot be shared. Shorten it, save, and try again.

## Receive an item

Any of these opens it in Shot Notes:

- scan the code with the Camera app, and tap what it shows;
- tap the link in a message or an email;
- copy the link, open the **Kit** tab, tap **Add from a link** and paste it. It
  can have other words around it.

If tapping a link opens a web page and not Shot Notes, copy the link and paste
it as in the last item.

Shot Notes shows what arrived, and adds nothing until you tap the button at the
bottom.

![What a link holds: a lens from a manufacturer that is not in your kit yet](../screenshots/app-add-shared-kit-light.svg#only-light){ .phone loading=lazy }
![What a link holds: a lens from a manufacturer that is not in your kit yet](../screenshots/app-add-shared-kit-dark.svg#only-dark){ .phone loading=lazy }

- The item has a box. Untick it if you do not want it after all.
- **New manufacturer** means you have no manufacturer of that name (compared
  without regard to capitals or extra spaces). Tap **Change** to put the item
  under one of your own manufacturers instead, such as Leica for Leica Camera
  AG.
- The button says how many items it adds: **Add 1 item**.

If your kit has the item already, with the same kind, manufacturer and name,
and for a lens the same focal lengths, the page says **Already in your kit**
and the box is not ticked, so opening the same link twice adds nothing.

![A lens that is already in the kit, with its box not ticked](../screenshots/app-add-shared-kit-already-light.svg#only-light){ .phone loading=lazy }
![A lens that is already in the kit, with its box not ticked](../screenshots/app-add-shared-kit-already-dark.svg#only-dark){ .phone loading=lazy }

A link can hold more than one item, and then the page lists them all, each with
its own box. The app shares one item at a time for now.

What you add is a copy: ordinary kit that you can edit, with no tie to the
sender's. A link that is damaged, that is not from Shot Notes or that is from a
newer version of the app adds nothing, and says so. If it is from a newer
version, ask the sender, or update the app.

A person who opens the link without Shot Notes sees a page on the
[website](https://www.shotnotes.app/kit/) that says what it is.

## Privacy

What the code and the link hold is in the part of the link after the `#`, which
a browser never sends to a website. Shot Notes sends nothing itself: the link
goes only where you send it. Anyone who has the link or the code can read what
it holds, as with a photograph you send, so share it with people you mean to.
