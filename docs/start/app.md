# Install the app

The app runs on **iPhone** and on **Android phones**. It is free, and needs no
account.

!!! note "Beta, for testers only"

    Shot Notes is in beta. The app is available only to testers, who are
    invited, and is not in the app stores yet. Testing is about to start.
    [Announcements](https://github.com/shotnotes/support/discussions/categories/announcements)
    will say when and how to join.

## What it needs

- **iPhone:** iOS 15 or later.
- **Android:** Android 7.0 or later.

These are the oldest versions the app is built to run on.

## Install it

As a tester:

- **iPhone:** open the invitation on the phone, install Apple's **TestFlight**
  app if you do not have it, and accept the invitation there. Then tap
  **Install** next to Shot Notes.
- **Android:** open the invitation on the phone, accept it, and install Shot
  Notes from Google Play, where the link takes you.

When the app is in the stores, search for **Shot Notes** in the App Store or in
Google Play, and install it as any other app.

Open it. The **Shots** tab is empty, and **+** asks you to add a lens first, so
that you choose it, instead of typing it, each time you log a shot.

## What it asks for

- **Location**, only when you tap **Use current location** on a shot. The phone
  asks the first time. Without it, you can still type the place.
- Nothing else. There is no account, no sign-in, no contacts, no photos and no
  notifications. [Settings and your data](../app/settings.md) says what leaves
  the phone, and when.

## Before the first shot

1. Open **Kit**, tap **+ Lens**, choose or add its manufacturer, and give the
   lens name and its focal length. The aperture range is optional.
2. Open **Settings**, and under **Carry over** choose what a new shot takes from
   the last one, such as the lens and the aperture.
3. Check that the phone sets its date and time **automatically**. The writer
   matches your notes to the photos by their times.

Then continue with [Your first shots](first-shots.md), or read
[Logging a shot](../app/shots.md).

## Updating and removing

Updates come through the store, and your shots stay. The shots are kept on the
phone only, so **export** them before you delete the app, if you want a copy.
